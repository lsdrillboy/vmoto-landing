Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

This policy describes how CANVAS SPV Co., Ltd (52/57 Moo 1, Na Jomtien Subdistrict, Sattahip District, Chonburi Province 20250, Thailand — «we», «VMOTO») handles personal data collected through the website vmotobikes.com.

1. What we collect

  • Data you submit: name, phone number, preferred messenger and location — when you send the contact form.
  • Technical data: cookies and similar identifiers set by analytics and advertising tools (Meta Pixel), your language preference stored in your browser (localStorage), IP address and device information processed by our hosting provider.

2. Why we use it

  • To respond to your request, arrange a test ride, delivery or service.
  • To keep records of enquiries in our customer system (CRM).
  • To measure advertising performance and show relevant ads (Meta Pixel events such as page views, form submissions and messenger clicks).

3. Who processes the data

We use trusted service providers acting on our behalf: Bitrix24 (CRM), Meta Platforms (advertising analytics), Vercel (website hosting). We do not sell personal data and do not share it with anyone else.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiry data is kept for as long as needed to handle your request and maintain our customer relationship, or until you ask us to delete it.

5. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time — message us on WhatsApp at +66 96 224 4666 or call the same number.

6. Cookies

The site uses cookies and localStorage for the language switcher and for Meta Pixel analytics. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the website will keep working.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
