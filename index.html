FAQ

Questions before buying

01 Can foreigners buy a bike? +

Yes — foreigners can officially buy and own a VMoto. We handle all the paperwork.

02 Registration and plates? +

We take care of the registration and plates ourselves right after the purchase.

03 Do I need a licence? +

Yes, same as any scooter: a Thai or an international motorcycle licence. We’ll advise how to get a local one.

04 Can I charge at home? +

Yes — each battery charges from a regular outlet with the included charger. A fast charge at our stations takes about 20 minutes.

05 Where is the bike serviced? +

On the island: our own service, parts in stock and a mobile team for Samui and Phangan. An electric drive needs no oil, belts or spark plugs.

06 How does a test ride work? +

We meet on Koh Phangan — you come to us, or we arrange a convenient spot individually. 15–20 minutes, free, no obligations.
